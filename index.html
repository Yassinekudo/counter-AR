How it works

  1. Aim at the shelfHold the phone 0.5 to 2 m away so a whole row fits on screen.
  2. Tap one productThe app outlines it. If the box is off, drag a box around the product instead.
  3. Name itThe name floats above the product in AR and is saved to your library.
  4. Sweep slowlyEvery matching item gets a numbered pin that stays put as you move. Items you have passed still count.
  5. Fix and saveTap a wrong item to remove it, tap a missed one to add it, then press Save.
